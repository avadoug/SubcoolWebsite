# Contributing archive material

Useful submissions include cultivar photographs, seed-pack scans, catalogs, advertisements, grow reports, correction evidence, and first-person memories.

Please provide:

- contributor name or handle;
- cultivar and approximate date;
- how the material was obtained;
- photographer/scanner and original owner where known;
- permission to display and the exact license/conditions;
- a short provenance story;
- whether private identity or location details must remain confidential.

Submissions pass spam, rights, and evidence review. Acceptance does not guarantee immediate publication. The archive will not expose private genetic holders or precise grow locations without permission. Takedown, privacy, and ownership disputes retain an internal action record even when public media is removed.
