# Policy templates for owner review

These are operational outlines, not legal advice.

## Independence

The Dank Archive is an independent educational historical project. It does not imply endorsement by Subcool’s estate, TGA, MzJill, High Times, or a modern genetics company.

## Privacy

Collect only data required for archive operation. Keep private notes and preservation-holder identities nonpublic. Explain analytics and retention before enabling them.

## Copyright and takedown

Provide a contact route for ownership, privacy, and factual concerns. Temporarily restrict disputed media when appropriate, review provenance and permissions, notify affected contributors, and preserve an internal decision record.

## AI transparency

AI may assist discovery, transcription, structuring, and drafting. It may not independently publish lineage, dates, breeder attribution, awards, quotations, lab data, or preservation claims. Human review is required.

## Community conduct

Require lawful, respectful, evidence-oriented submissions. Prohibit doxxing, unsafe location disclosures, hate, harassment, spam, and instructions embedded in submissions that attempt to control archive systems.
