# Editorial policy

## Evidence order

Subcool-authored material and contemporary official TGA records lead; contemporary interviews and documented collaborators follow; catalogs and reputable archives corroborate; retailers and modern databases are leads, not automatic fact.

## Claim states

- Documented: direct near-primary evidence.
- Strongly supported: credible independent evidence converges.
- Supported: useful evidence exists but needs stronger corroboration.
- Community reported: repeated observation, not breeder fact.
- Anecdotal: historically interesting but unverified.
- Disputed / unresolved: credible conflict or insufficient evidence.
- Superseded / rejected: retained for history but not canonical.

Only senior-curator review may change a canonical claim state. AI-assisted work enters draft/researching and cannot become published without a named human reviewer and decision record.

## Corrections

A correction must identify the record and claim, propose a value, and supply evidence. It enters moderation; it never overwrites the public record directly. Major changes preserve the old value, reasoning, date, and reviewer.

## Sensory and health language

Aroma, flavor, cultivation, and effect descriptions are attributed observations. The archive does not turn anecdote into medical advice or fabricate laboratory values. “Reliable laboratory data unavailable” is an acceptable result.

## Images

No downloaded photograph publishes without creator, source, rights state, license, and identity status. Botanical references remain labeled. Fake historical photographs and unlicensed seed-bank/forum images are prohibited.
